# WoT AV v0.1 - compact publication note

**Original proposal, not a W3C vocabulary or deployment.** `https://example.org/wot/av#` remains unregistered; `https://example.org/wot/av/context/v0.1` remains an unhosted external-context placeholder. All release files use the new prefix `media-research\wot-av-release-v0.1`. The six formal-source files are unchanged.

**Core choice.** Retain all **38 classes, 105 AV properties and 17 reused external properties**. Add only the controlled individuals `av:H265` and `av:Opus`; no new classes or properties. The compact context retains every original mapping, including `@set`, ordered `@list` preferences and scalar coercions. Class names resolve through the existing `av` prefix. The Turtle graph retains every original triple and adds only the two codec definitions and their primary citations. The complete machine catalogue preserves every original definition, domain/range/cardinality, unit, profile obligation and admission/lifecycle rule.

**Codec semantics.** `av:H265` identifies the ITU-T H.265 / ISO/IEC 23008-2 HEVC bitstream family, not H.264, a container or WebRTC support. Its exact edition, profile/tier/level, constraints and parameter-set initialization require a recognized pinned configuration.[1] `av:Opus` identifies the RFC 6716 interactive speech/audio coding family, not PCM, a container or a transport.[2] Neither IRI proves encoding, decoding, browser or device support. IANA `video/H265` and `audio/opus` registrations are references, not RDF equivalences or defaults for native Form `contentType`.

**Examples.** `media-research\wot-av-release-v0.1.examples.jsonld` contains a 1920x1080 H.265 Track at exact Rational 30/1 and a joint WebRTC Mode with H.264 video plus Opus audio. These are synthetic description fragments, **not complete TDs or admission-ready plans**: named native Forms and configuration pins remain deliberately unresolved. Track is the class; `av:representation` takes an IRI value such as `av:EncodedVideo` or `av:EncodedAudio`. The WebRTC selection illustrates a negotiated combination, not a complete mandatory-codec implementation; H.265 is not made mandatory by RFC 7742.[3] Opus RTP's 48000 Hz clock and SDP `opus/48000/2` do not establish actual sampling rate or channel count. The example's explicit 48000-sample, stereo media signature is illustrative, not a default.[2]

**Native WoT and control.** `namedForms` remains the canonical reference-mechanism label: absolute native Form `@id`, `av:form`, a pinned TD, one standard operation, and an optional agreeing JSON Pointer. Existing Form metadata stays native; no competing endpoint or copied `href` is introduced.[4] Keep singular `av:offer`, `av:mode`, `av:track`, `av:input`, `av:alternative`, and Rational `av:numerator`/`av:denominator`. Semantic control roles are identifiers/labels mapped to native affordances/FormReferences by the existing recognized, pinned control profile and its exact schemas. They are not new core AV action classes or operations.

**Controlled-set evolution.** Unknown required codecs, parameters or profiles still cannot satisfy admission. A future recognized versioned profile must explicitly declare additional IRIs, immutable meanings, primary sources, representation applicability and exact configuration/schema/matching obligations. Publish and pin its extended inventory and validators with their dependency closure; AV-namespace additions need a future authored AV catalogue. Recognition and complete-plan qualification are explicit, not inferred from prefix expansion. Existing meanings, coercions, cardinalities and TD operations remain unchanged; an older pinned release keeps its original closed sets. No silent fallback, nearest-codec substitution or speculative codec expansion is permitted.

**Publication evidence.** Installed PyLD and RDFLib check JSON-LD expansion, all property coercions/containers, class/property coverage, RDF isomorphism, complete definitions and unknown-term detection. `media-research\wot-av-release-v0.1.checks.json` records `context_catalogue_exact_coverage`, `original_graph_preserved_except_codec_additions`, and `no_unknown_av_runtime_terms`. SHACL is explicitly **candidate structural material**: only its three codec lists change. No SHACL engine, full shape-syntax, admission, hardware or protocol conformance is claimed; independent validation of the original shapes remains separate. External artifact hashes exclude the manifest's own hash.
